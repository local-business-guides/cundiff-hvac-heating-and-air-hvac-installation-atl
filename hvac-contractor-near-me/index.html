What to Ask About Service, Timing, and Cost

- Typical timing: Timing depends on the service and the condition of the existing system. Ask when the work could begin and how long the proposed scope is expected to take.
- Cost factors: The type of equipment, the problem or work identified, access to the system, and any additional work can affect cost. Ask what the assessment includes and request the expected charges before authorizing work.
